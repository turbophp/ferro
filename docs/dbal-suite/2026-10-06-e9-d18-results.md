## Not established

- **D18's reading is clause 3, per the §21 open item.** "GREEN modulo triage" means every difference
  from the control is a triaged driver-name artifact or a documented incompatibility (clause 3). It is
  not "green" until the owner settles whether D18 means clause 1.
- **Strict pass parity is reported, not gated.** No DBAL column meets it (table above); the gate prints
  it beside the verdict and it never fails a job.
- **The ORM workflow (`orm-suite`) has no triage gate.** It was not part of this dispatch; its columns
  still use `compare-columns.php`'s two-argument report mode.
- **Upstream Laravel's `Sqlite/` directory is not run** (the companion record,
  `docs/laravel-suite/2026-10-06-e9-d18-results.md`).
- The measured artifact is the curated `testkit/dbal/allowlist.txt` subset, not upstream's whole
  functional suite, as at every earlier DBAL record.
- The job logs were read through the GitHub API's job-log endpoint; the uploaded artifacts (JUnit,
  `d18.txt`, `ferrod.log`) were not downloaded from this environment.
